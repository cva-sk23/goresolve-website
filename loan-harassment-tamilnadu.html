How to stop loan harassment legally

  • File a complaint with the Banking Ombudsman
  • Report violations of RBI recovery guidelines
  • Maintain call recordings and proof of harassment
  • Send a legal notice if harassment continues

Your rights under RBI guidelines

Under RBI rules, recovery agents cannot use abusive language, threaten borrowers, or visit at inappropriate hours. Document every incident and use the regulator’s complaint channels to seek relief.

Key action: Lodge a formal complaint with your lender and copy the RBI Consumer Education & Protection Department. If unresolved, escalate to the Banking Ombudsman immediately.

Why prompt action matters

Delay allows unlawful collection activity to continue and may harm your credit history indirectly. GOresolve works with experts who handle quick negotiation, communication, and legal escalation for you.

Immediate steps

  • Note names, timings, and content of calls or visits.
  • Send a legal notice through registered mail.
  • Demand all communication be in writing.
  • Seek compensation for mental harassment where applicable.

Tip: Use the RBI Ombudsman’s online portal at https://www.rbi.org.in to track your complaint and keep deadlines from lapsing.
