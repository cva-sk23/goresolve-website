Complaint Against Bank India

This page explains how to register complaints against banks and seek resolution in India.

Updated: April 10, 2026 Reading Time: 5 min

When Should You File a Complaint Against Your Bank?

You have the right to file a complaint against your bank when they have treated you unfairly, charged you wrongly, or failed to resolve your issue after 30 days. Common reasons include wrongful loan charges, harassment by recovery agents, incorrect CIBIL reporting, denial of NOC, or failure to process settlement.

Rule: Always complain to your bank first in writing. If they don't respond in 30 days — or give an unsatisfactory response — you can escalate to the Banking Ombudsman for free.

Step-by-Step: How to File Complaint Against Bank in India

1

Write to the Bank's Grievance Cell

Every bank must have a designated grievance redressal officer. Email or write to them with your complaint number, account details, and a clear description of the issue. Keep a copy of everything.

2

Wait 30 Days for Response

Banks are required to respond within 30 days. If they don't respond or the response is unsatisfactory, you are eligible to escalate to the Banking Ombudsman.

3

File at cms.rbi.org.in — Free

Go to the RBI Complaint Management System. Select your bank, describe the issue, upload documents. No fee. No lawyer needed. You get a tracking number immediately.

4

Consumer Court (Optional)

For deficiency of service, you can also file in Consumer Court. Filing fee is ₹100–₹5,000 depending on claim amount. Consumer courts can award compensation including mental agony damages.

Complaints You Can File Against Banks

  • Wrongful charges, hidden fees, or unexplained deductions
  • Recovery agent harassment — calls at odd hours, threats, abuse
  • Wrong CIBIL reporting after settlement
  • Refusal to issue NOC or loan closure certificate
  • Unfair loan restructuring or settlement rejection
  • Cheque or ECS bounce without valid reason
  • Failure to follow RBI Fair Practice Code
  • Discrimination or unfair loan rejection

Important: Always document everything. Screenshot calls, save all SMSes, keep written records of every interaction. Evidence is what makes your complaint strong.

How to Make Your Complaint Strong

  • Include your loan account number and customer ID
  • Mention specific dates of the incidents
  • Attach bank statements, call recordings, or SMS screenshots
  • State clearly what resolution you want — refund, NOC, CIBIL correction
  • Keep the language factual — avoid emotional language

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I file a complaint against an NBFC?

Yes. NBFCs registered with RBI fall under the Banking Ombudsman scheme. You can file at cms.rbi.org.in against NBFCs for harassment, wrong charges, or unfair recovery.

What if the bank ignores my complaint?

If the bank does not respond within 30 days, go directly to the Banking Ombudsman. Non-response is itself a valid ground for complaint.

Can I get compensation from the bank?

Yes. The Banking Ombudsman can award compensation up to ₹1 lakh for harassment and mental agony, and up to ₹20 lakh for financial loss.
