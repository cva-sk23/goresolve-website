BANKING OMBUDSMAN — FEE & CHARGES

Is Banking Ombudsman Free? Fee Charged Explained India 2026

Many borrowers hesitate to file a Banking Ombudsman complaint thinking it costs money. The answer is simple — it costs nothing. Here is everything you need to know.

Updated:May 2026 Reading Time: 4 min
₹0 — Completely Free

The Banking Ombudsman charges zero fee to file a complaint. No registration fee. No processing fee. No lawyer required. It is a free service provided by RBI for all bank customers in India.

What is the Fee Charged by Banking Ombudsman?

The fee charged by the Banking Ombudsman for filing a complaint is absolutely zero. The RBI Banking Ombudsman Scheme 2006 was created specifically to give ordinary bank customers a free and fast way to resolve disputes with banks, NBFCs, and loan apps — without needing lawyers or courts.

This is confirmed directly in RBI's official scheme: "No fee shall be charged for filing a complaint."

Official answer: The fee charged by Banking Ombudsman for filing a complaint in India is ₹0. It is free for all bank customers, borrowers, and depositors.

Who Can File for Free?

Any individual who is a customer of a bank, NBFC, or digital lending app can file a complaint for free. This includes:

  • Borrowers facing loan harassment or wrongful recovery
  • Customers with wrongful charges or hidden fees
  • People whose loan settlement was not honoured
  • Anyone facing unfair EMI deductions
  • Customers denied loan documents or NOC

How to File Banking Ombudsman Complaint for Free

1

First complain to your bank

File a written complaint with your bank's grievance cell. Wait 30 days for their response. If no reply or unsatisfactory reply, proceed to Ombudsman.

2

Visit cms.rbi.org.in

Go to the RBI Complaint Management System. Click "File a Complaint." Select your bank and category of complaint. No login required for first-time users.

3

Fill the complaint form

Describe your problem clearly. Mention dates, amounts, and what the bank did wrong. Attach any supporting documents — bank statements, letters, screenshots.

4

Submit and track

You get a complaint number immediately. Track status online at any time. The Ombudsman contacts the bank on your behalf — you do nothing further.

5

Resolution in 30–90 days

Most complaints are resolved within 30 days. If the bank is found at fault, you can receive compensation up to ₹20 lakh — all at zero cost to you.

Are There Any Hidden Charges?

No. There are absolutely no hidden charges in the Banking Ombudsman process. Beware of agents or websites claiming to file your Ombudsman complaint for a fee — this is unnecessary and potentially fraudulent.

Warning: If anyone asks you to pay money to file a Banking Ombudsman complaint, it is a scam. The official process at cms.rbi.org.in is always free.

What Can You Get as Compensation?

If the Ombudsman rules in your favour, the bank must:

  • Refund wrongful charges or deductions
  • Pay compensation for harassment — up to ₹1 lakh for mental agony
  • Pay up to ₹20 lakh for financial loss
  • Correct your CIBIL record if wrongly reported
  • Issue NOC or loan closure documents if wrongly withheld

When Ombudsman Cannot Help

The Banking Ombudsman cannot take up complaints that:

  • Are already in court or consumer forum
  • Are more than one year old from when the bank rejected your complaint
  • Involve amounts above ₹20 lakh (use civil court instead)
  • Are against non-regulated lenders (private moneylenders)

Pro tip: Always get a written rejection from your bank before going to Ombudsman. A verbal "we can't help you" is not enough — demand a written response.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there any fee for Banking Ombudsman in India?

No. The Banking Ombudsman scheme is completely free. RBI provides this as a free grievance redressal service for all bank customers.

Do I need a lawyer to file Banking Ombudsman complaint?

No. You can file the complaint yourself online at cms.rbi.org.in. No lawyer, advocate, or agent is required.

What is the fee charged by Banking Ombudsman for filing a complaint?

Zero. The fee charged by the Banking Ombudsman for filing a complaint is ₹0. It has always been free since the scheme was launched.

Can I file against an NBFC or loan app?

Yes. NBFCs registered with RBI and digital lending apps regulated by RBI fall under the Ombudsman scheme. You can file against them for free.

Where do I file Banking Ombudsman complaint?

You can file directly through RBI's official Complaint Management System at cms.rbi.org.in.
