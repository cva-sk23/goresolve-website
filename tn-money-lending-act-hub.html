1. What is the Tamil Nadu Money Lending Act 2025?

The Tamil Nadu Money Lending Entities Act 2025 is landmark state legislation designed to regulate money lenders, protect borrowers from harassment, and establish clear legal boundaries for debt recovery in Tamil Nadu.

Key Fact: This act applies to private money lenders, loan apps, and recovery agencies. It adds state-level protection for Tamil Nadu residents on top of RBI guidelines for banks.

Why Was This Act Created?

Between 2020-2024, Tamil Nadu saw a surge in recovery agent harassment:

  • Aggressive late-night calls & home visits
  • Public shaming in front of family and colleagues
  • Threats, intimidation, and criminal language
  • Illegal asset seizures without court orders
  • Targeting of guarantors without consent

2. Your Legal Rights as a Borrower

✅ Right 1: Protection from Harassment

Recovery agents cannot use abusive, threatening, or demeaning language. No caste-based slurs, threats to you or your family, or public defamation.

✅ Right 2: The "8 AM to 7 PM" Rule

Home visits only between 8 AM-7 PM, weekdays only. No Sunday or holiday visits. No entry without explicit permission.

✅ Right 3: No Workplace Contact

Recovery agents cannot visit/call you at work without written consent. Exceptions only if employer is a co-guarantor.

✅ Right 4: Right to Know Who's Calling

Agents must identify themselves before contact. Cannot hide identity or pretend to be government officials.

✅ Right 5: Guarantor Protection

Family members cannot be pressured unless they're officially named guarantors on the loan. No blame or threats to family.

4. The "8 AM to 7 PM" Rule Explained

✅ What Recovery Agents CAN Do

  • Call your phone between 8 AM-7 PM on weekdays
  • Send written notices via registered mail
  • Visit your home on weekdays at 11 AM with notice
  • Contact your employer (if named guarantor)

❌ What Recovery Agents CANNOT Do

  • Call at 8 PM or before 8 AM
  • Visit doorstep at 8:30 PM
  • Call on Sundays or national holidays
  • Show up unannounced at workplace
  • Send threatening messages after 7 PM
⚠️ Real Case (Chennai, 2025): A borrower received an agent visit at 8 PM with threats to seize his TV. He filed a police complaint under Article 21. Recovery agency ordered to pay ₹50,000 damages.

5. How to File a Police Complaint

Option 1: Online Complaint (Fast)

  1. Visit www.tnpolice.gov.in
  2. Click "Citizen Services" → "File Complaint"
  3. Select "Economic Offence" or "Harassment"
  4. Upload evidence (WhatsApp screenshots, call logs)
  5. Submit & get reference number
  6. Police will contact within 24-48 hours

Option 2: Visit Police Station

Bring with you:

  • Loan agreement copy
  • WhatsApp chat screenshots
  • Call logs from phone provider
  • Recordings of threatening calls (if any)
  • ID proof and address proof

What to Say at Police Station:

"I am filing an FIR for recovery agent harassment under TN Money Lending Act 2025. They violated the law by [describe specific violations: calls after 7 PM, abusive language, threats]. I have evidence."

Pro Tip: File police complaint BEFORE settling. This protects you legally and strengthens your negotiating position with the lender.

6. Escalating to RBI Banking Ombudsman

If your lender is a bank, escalate to the RBI Banking Ombudsman office in Chennai.

When to Escalate?

  • Bank's Nodal Officer didn't respond in 30 days
  • You received harassment from bank's recovery team
  • Bank charging illegal fees or interest
  • Bank threatening legal action improperly

7. Taking Legal Action

If harassment continues, file a civil suit for damages:

  • Defamation: If publicly shamed, claim ₹1,00,000+
  • Invasion of Privacy: Home/workspace violations, claim ₹50,000+
  • Emotional Distress: Document mental health impact, claim ₹2,00,000+
  • Wrongful Action: Property seizure, claim full value + damages

8. FAQs on Your Legal Protections

Q: Can recovery agents call my mobile every day between 8 AM-7 PM? ▼
They can call on weekdays 8 AM-7 PM, but excessive calls (5+/day) constitute harassment. Limit is 2-3 calls per week under RBI guidelines.
Q: Can I record a recovery agent's call? ▼
Yes. Under IT Act and TN laws, you can record calls for evidence of harassment. At least one party (you) must know about the recording.
Q: If I file a police complaint, will my lender sue me? ▼
The police complaint protects you legally. Your lender can still file a debt recovery suit, but harassment complaint is separate legal action with different rules.
Q: How long does RBI Ombudsman take to resolve? ▼
Typically 60-90 days. The ombudsman is free, doesn't require a lawyer, and has strong powers to order banks to compensate you.
Q: What if recovery agent claims to be from police/CBI? ▼
This is fraud (IPC §419, 420). It's illegal. File an FIR immediately. This false representation can result in imprisonment up to 3 years.
