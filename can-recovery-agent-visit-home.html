Can Recovery Agent Visit Your Home in India? Know Your Rights 2026

A recovery agent showed up at your door — or you are worried they will. Here is exactly what RBI rules say about home visits, calling times, and what you can do if they cross the line.

Updated: April 13, 2026 Reading Time: 5 min

Can a Recovery Agent Visit Your Home?

Yes — but only under strict conditions. RBI's Fair Practice Code and the 2022 Digital Lending Guidelines permit recovery agents to visit a borrower's residence. However, they must follow very specific rules. Violating any of these rules is illegal and can be reported.

RBI Rule: Recovery agents may visit the borrower's place of residence or business — but only between 7:00 AM and 7:00 PM, without intimidation, abuse, or threat of any kind.

What Recovery Agents Can and Cannot Do During Home Visits

✅ Allowed

  • Visit between 7AM and 7PM
  • Identify themselves by name and bank
  • Show their authorisation letter
  • Discuss outstanding dues calmly
  • Request payment or repayment plan
  • Leave a written notice

🚫 Not Allowed

  • Visit before 7AM or after 7PM
  • Use abusive or threatening language
  • Intimidate family members
  • Create a scene in public or at door
  • Seize any personal property
  • Refuse to show authorisation
  • Contact neighbours or relatives

What to Do When a Recovery Agent Comes to Your Home

1

Stay calm — do not panic

You are protected by RBI rules. They cannot force entry, seize your belongings, or threaten you. Opening or not opening the door is your right.

2

Ask for their authorisation letter

Every recovery agent must carry a written authorisation from the bank. Ask to see it. Note their name, employee ID, and the bank's name. They are legally required to provide this.

3

Record the interaction

If possible, record the conversation on your phone. This becomes evidence if they misbehave. Inform them you are recording — this alone often stops misconduct immediately.

4

Do not hand over any cash at the door

Never pay a recovery agent directly in cash without a proper receipt. All payments should go through your official loan account. Door-step cash collection is a red flag.

5

File a complaint if they misbehave

If they threaten, abuse, visit at wrong hours, or cause a scene — file a complaint immediately. You have multiple options to escalate.

How to Stop Illegal Recovery Agent Home Visits

If agents are visiting at wrong hours, threatening you, or behaving illegally, here is how to stop them:

  • Write to the bank's nodal officer — every bank must have one. Send a written complaint with dates and details of the visit.
  • File complaint at cms.rbi.org.in — the RBI Complaint Management System. Completely free. Agents must stop while complaint is under review.
  • File police complaint — if threats or intimidation occurred, file an FIR under IPC Section 503 (criminal intimidation) and Section 506.
  • Send a legal notice — a formal legal notice to the bank warning them of further action often stops harassment within days.

Important: If a recovery agent visits after 7PM, uses abusive language, threatens your family, or refuses to show ID — this is illegal under RBI rules. Document everything and file a complaint the same day.

What If They Come to Your Workplace?

Recovery agents visiting your workplace face the same restrictions — 7AM to 7PM only, no intimidation, no public scenes. Additionally, they must not discuss your debt with your employer or colleagues. If they do, this is a privacy violation and grounds for a serious complaint.

Can They Visit Your Family Members?

No. Recovery agents cannot visit, call, or contact your family members, neighbours, or friends regarding your loan. Your debt is your private matter. Contacting third parties to embarrass or pressure you is explicitly prohibited under RBI's 2022 Digital Lending Guidelines and the earlier Fair Practice Code.

Know this: If a recovery agent contacts your family members, spouse, parents, or colleagues about your loan — that is a serious RBI violation. File an immediate complaint at cms.rbi.org.in and with the bank's nodal officer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a recovery agent come to my house at night?

No. RBI rules strictly prohibit recovery agents from visiting your home before 7AM or after 7PM. Any visit outside these hours is illegal.

What time can recovery agents call — the DRA calling time?

Recovery agents (DRAs) can only call between 7:00 AM and 7:00 PM. Calls before 7AM or after 7PM violate RBI's Fair Practice Code. You can file a complaint for this violation.

Can I refuse entry to a recovery agent?

Yes. You have no legal obligation to let a recovery agent into your home. They cannot force entry. If they try, call the police immediately.

What if a recovery agent refuses to show ID?

Refuse to engage with them. Every authorised recovery agent must carry and show a letter of authorisation from the bank. Without it, you have no obligation to speak with them.
