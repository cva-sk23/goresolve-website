What Loan App Harassment Looks Like in Chennai

Loan apps — particularly unregulated instant loan apps — use a specific playbook to intimidate borrowers in Chennai. These tactics are designed to make you pay out of panic and embarrassment before you realise they are illegal:

⚠️ Common Illegal Tactics Used by Loan Apps

  • Sending threatening WhatsApp messages to contacts from your phone book
  • Creating fake "legal notices" and sending them via WhatsApp
  • Calling your employer, relatives, or neighbours to report your "fraud"
  • Morphing your photo on fake "criminal wanted" posters and circulating them
  • Threatening FIR and arrest for non-payment of a civil debt
  • Sending messages at midnight, 2 AM, or outside 8 AM–7 PM — a direct RBI violation
  • Charging interest rates of 200–400% annually — far above RBI guidelines

Every single one of the above is either an RBI guideline violation, a violation of the IT Act, or in many cases a criminal offence under the TN Money Lending Entities Act 2025.

Is the Loan App Even Legal? How to Check

Many loan apps operating in Chennai are either unregistered NBFCs or Chinese-backed apps operating through shell companies. Before responding to any demands, verify:

1

Check RBI's registered NBFC list

Go to rbi.org.in → Financial Education → RBI Kehta Hai or search the RBI's NBFC registration database. If the app's parent company is not a registered NBFC, every loan they offered and every rupee they collected may be illegal.

2

Check if they are on RBI's alert list

RBI periodically publishes a list of unauthorised digital lenders. Check: rbi.org.in → Consumer Education → Alert List. Many popular harassment apps are already listed there.

3

Check Google Play Store removal status

Google has removed hundreds of predatory loan apps from the Play Store. If the app is no longer on the Play Store, it is almost certainly unregulated.

Your Rights — What Loan Apps Cannot Legally Do

📵

Cannot contact your family

Contacting third parties without written consent is prohibited under RBI's Fair Practices Code and the IT Act.

⏰

Cannot call outside 8AM–7PM

Any contact — call, SMS, WhatsApp — outside permitted hours is a direct RBI violation.

🚫

Cannot threaten arrest

Loan default is a civil matter. No loan app can file an FIR or have you arrested for non-payment.

🖼️

Cannot morph or share your photo

Photo morphing and circulation is a criminal offence under Section 67 of the IT Act — punishable with imprisonment.

📋

Cannot access all your contacts

RBI mandates that apps may only access contacts necessary for their stated purpose. Bulk access for harassment is illegal.

💸

Cannot charge unlimited interest

Registered NBFCs must follow RBI's interest rate guidelines. Unregulated apps charging 200–400% APR are operating illegally.

Step-by-Step: How to Fight Back Against Loan App Harassment

1

Screenshot everything immediately

Screenshot every threatening message, every contact notification, every call log. Take screen recordings if messages are being sent to your contacts. Do this before you do anything else — evidence disappears when apps are uninstalled.

2

Revoke the app's contact access

Go to your phone Settings → Apps → [Loan App] → Permissions → Revoke Contacts access. Do this for every loan app on your phone. Also revoke Camera, Microphone, and Location permissions.

3

File a cybercrime complaint online

Go to cybercrime.gov.in and file a complaint under "Financial Fraud" and/or "Cyber Stalking / Harassment". The National Cybercrime Helpline is 1930. Chennai Cybercrime Police Station is at Vepery — you can also visit in person with your screenshots.

4

File an RBI complaint if the lender is a registered NBFC

If the lender is a registered NBFC, file at cms.rbi.org.in. The RBI Ombudsman can direct the lender to stop harassment and pay compensation.

5

Report to Google Play / Apple App Store

Report the app to Google Play using the "Flag as inappropriate" feature. Include screenshots of threatening messages. Google has removed apps based on such reports.

6

Send a written response to the lender

Write to the app's registered email address (find it in the app's Play Store listing or website). State clearly: (a) you are aware of your rights, (b) their actions are violations of the RBI code and IT Act, (c) you have filed complaints with cybercrime and RBI, and (d) you are willing to repay the legitimate principal but will not pay under coercion.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I pay just to make the harassment stop?

No — panic payments often make things worse. Unregulated apps interpret immediate payment as confirmation that their tactics work, leading to more demands. Stopping harassment requires legal action and formal complaints, not payment under coercion.

Can the loan app actually file a police case against me?

Loan default is a civil matter — not criminal. No legitimate lender can file an FIR for non-payment of a loan. If an agent claims they will get you arrested, they are lying. If they actually approach police, the police are required to refuse an FIR for a civil debt matter.

What if they have already messaged my employer?

This is a serious violation. File an immediate complaint at cybercrime.gov.in and the RBI Ombudsman. Contact GOresolve — we send formal legal notices to lenders on your behalf that typically stop such contact within 24–48 hours.
