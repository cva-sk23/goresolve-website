Banking Ombudsman Complaint India (2026) – How to File RBI Complaint Against Bank

If you're facing issues with a bank and customer support isn't helping, you can file a Banking Ombudsman complaint. The Banking Ombudsman is part of the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) and helps resolve complaints between customers and banks.

Updated: April 10, 2026 Reading Time: 8 min

What is Banking Ombudsman?

The Banking Ombudsman is a grievance redressal system established by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI). It helps customers resolve complaints against:

  • Banks
  • NBFCs
  • Loan apps
  • Digital lenders
  • Credit card companies

The best part:

  • ✅ Free service
  • ✅ No lawyer required
  • ✅ Online complaint
  • ✅ RBI monitored resolution

This makes Banking Ombudsman one of the most powerful tools for borrowers.

When Can You File Banking Ombudsman Complaint?

You can file a complaint when:

  • Bank not responding
  • Recovery harassment
  • Wrong charges
  • Loan settlement issues
  • Credit card disputes
  • Unauthorized transactions
  • Delay in refund
  • EMI issues

Most borrowers file complaints for:

  • Loan harassment
  • Settlement disputes
  • Credit card issues
  • Recovery agent harassment

When You Cannot File Complaint

You cannot file complaint if:

  • Case already in court
  • Complaint older than 1 year
  • You didn't contact bank first

Important Rule:

👉 You must first complain to bank
👉 Wait 30 days
👉 Then file Ombudsman complaint

This is mandatory.

How to File Banking Ombudsman Complaint (Step-by-Step)

Follow this process:

1

Contact Bank First

Send email to bank: Customer care, Nodal officer, Grievance team. Keep proof of email.

2

Wait 30 Days

If bank doesn't respond, rejects complaint, or gives unsatisfactory response, then move to RBI.

3

File RBI Complaint

You can file complaint online, email, or written letter. Online method is fastest.

Banking Ombudsman Online Complaint Process

Follow these steps:

  • Visit RBI complaint portal
  • Select complaint type
  • Enter bank details
  • Upload documents
  • Submit complaint

After submission:

  • Complaint number generated
  • RBI reviews case
  • Bank asked to respond

Documents Required for Banking Ombudsman Complaint

Keep these ready:

  • ID proof
  • Bank statements
  • Loan agreement
  • Email communication
  • Call records (if harassment)

Better documentation increases success rate.

Banking Ombudsman Complaint Time

Typical timeline:

  • Complaint filed → Day 1
  • RBI review → 7–10 days
  • Bank response → 15–30 days
  • Resolution → 30–60 days

Some cases resolve faster.

RBI Rules for Recovery Agents

Recovery agents cannot:

  • ❌ Call before 8 AM
  • ❌ Call after 7 PM
  • ❌ Threaten borrower
  • ❌ Contact relatives repeatedly
  • ❌ Use abusive language

If violated → File complaint immediately.
