⚖️ Your Rights Under RBI Guidelines

  • Recovery agents cannot call before 7 AM or after 7 PM
  • You cannot be threatened, abused, or harassed verbally
  • Agents cannot visit your workplace without prior notice
  • You have the right to a fair settlement negotiation
  • Lenders must provide a clear statement of dues on request
  • You can file a formal complaint with the Banking Ombudsman
  • Your family members cannot be contacted or harassed
GOresolve is 100% on the borrower’s side. We are not a collection agency and not a law firm. We never work for lenders or banks.
