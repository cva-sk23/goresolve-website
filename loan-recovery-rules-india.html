Loan Recovery Rules India

This page explains loan recovery rules and legal protections for borrowers in India.

Updated: April 10, 2026 Reading Time: 5 min

RBI Loan Recovery Rules — What the Law Says

The Reserve Bank of India has established clear rules for loan recovery through the Fair Practice Code (2006), the Master Circular on Recovery Agents (2007), and the Digital Lending Guidelines (2022). Every bank and NBFC must follow these rules — no exceptions.

Key rule: Recovery agents can only contact borrowers between 7:00 AM and 7:00 PM. Any contact outside this window is an RBI violation and grounds for complaint.

RBI Guidelines on Recovery Agent Training

Under RBI's Master Circular on Recovery Agents, every recovery agent must be trained and certified. Banks and NBFCs are responsible for the conduct of their agents. If an agent misbehaves, the lender is legally liable — not just the agent.

  • Agents must carry a photo ID and bank authorisation letter
  • They must identify themselves clearly before starting any conversation
  • They must provide the bank's grievance contact details if asked
  • Recorded calls must be maintained by the lender for audit purposes

New Loan Recovery Rules 2022 — What Changed

RBI's August 2022 Digital Lending Guidelines brought significant new protections for borrowers, especially for digital loans and app-based lending:

  • Contact list ban: Lending apps cannot access or use your phone's contact list for recovery
  • WhatsApp restrictions: Agents cannot send morphed images or threatening messages on any platform
  • Data deletion: Once a loan is repaid, the lender must delete your personal data
  • Single point of contact: Borrowers must be given one contact number for all queries
  • Cooling off period: Borrowers must be given time to reconsider before loan disbursal

If any of these rules are violated: File an immediate complaint at cms.rbi.org.in. The RBI takes violations of the 2022 Digital Lending Guidelines very seriously and has penalised multiple lenders for non-compliance.

How to File Complaint for Recovery Rule Violation

1

Document the violation with date and time

Note exact time of call, what was said, and any evidence. Call recordings, screenshots, and WhatsApp messages are all valid.

2

Write to the lender's nodal officer

Send a formal email stating the RBI rule violated, the date and time, and what action you expect. Most lenders respond within 48 hours when an RBI rule is cited specifically.

3

File at cms.rbi.org.in

Completely free. Select the lender, choose "Recovery Agent Harassment" as the category, and upload your evidence. You receive a complaint number for tracking.
