What You're Protected Against Under TN Law

❌ Abusive Language

Recovery agents cannot use threatening or demeaning language. Violation = Criminal penalty.

❌ After 7 PM Calls/Visits

No contact after 7 PM or before 8 AM. No Sunday visits. Your home is your sanctuary.

❌ Workplace Harassment

Recovery agents cannot contact you at work without permission. Protect your job security.

❌ Family Harassment

Your family members cannot be pressured unless they're official guarantors.
