Why I Built GOresolve

I am a Debt Recovery Agent certified by the Indian Institute of Banking & Finance (IIBF) — the same examination that banks require their own recovery agents to pass. I know how collections departments work from the inside: what banks can legally do, where they routinely cross the line, and what negotiation levers actually move the needle.

After working in this industry, I saw thousands of borrowers suffering under illegal harassment, fake legal notices, and intimidation tactics — all because they did not know their rights. Banks count on that ignorance.

GOresolve exists to close that gap. When a borrower has a certified professional who understands both sides of the table, the dynamic changes completely.

Every case we handle is guided by RBI rules, the TN Money Lending Entities Act 2025, and one principle: your financial distress should never cost you your dignity.

The GOresolve Difference

We Know How Banks Think — Because We've Worked That Side of the Table

Most borrower platforms are run by lawyers or financial advisors who have never sat inside a bank's collections department. Our founder is IIBF-certified — the exact standard banks hold their own recovery agents to.

That means we know when an agent is violating RBI's Fair Practices Code. We know which OTS percentages banks will realistically accept. We know how to write a settlement request letter that actually gets read.

This insider knowledge is what we bring to every borrower case across Chennai and Tamil Nadu.

The Problem GOresolve Solves

Borrowers face aggressive recovery calls, WhatsApp threats disguised as legal notices, and agents visiting after 7 PM. Most suffer in silence — unaware these are violations under RBI guidelines and the TN Money Lending Entities Act 2025.

Accounts that could settle at 40–60% of outstanding via OTS spiral into write-offs simply because no one guided the borrower through the process.

GOresolve was built to fix both problems. With a certified professional in your corner, you know your rights — and your options.
