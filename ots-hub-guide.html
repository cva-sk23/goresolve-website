What is One-Time Settlement?

A One-Time Settlement (OTS) is a bank's offer to close your loan permanently by accepting a reduced payment—meaning they forgive part of your debt in exchange for immediate cash recovery.

Key Fact: OTS is NOT full debt forgiveness. You still pay money, just significantly less than what you originally owed. Banks offer OTS to recover funds quickly without lengthy legal processes.

Why Do Banks Offer OTS?

  • Quick Cash Recovery: Rather than wait 2-3 years for legal proceedings, banks take a 50-70% haircut to recover funds immediately.
  • Reduced Bad Loan Provisioning: Unpaid loans are liabilities on the bank's balance sheet. OTS clears these liabilities.
  • Lower Collection Costs: Recovery agencies cost 15-20% of recovery. OTS eliminates this expense.
  • Legal Risk Management: Harassment cases make collection risky. OTS avoids liability.

How to Negotiate the Best OTS Deal

1

Document Your Hardship

Prepare evidence: job loss, medical bills, business closure. Banks always ask "Why should we waive?"

2

Start Conversations Early

Contact the bank's NPA Department. Say: "I want to settle. What's your best waiver for lump-sum payment?"

3

Make Your First Offer

Offer 30-40% of outstanding. They counter at 60%. You settle at ~45-50%.

4

Negotiate Based on Timing

Fiscal year-end (Feb-Mar) = better waivers. Banks want bad loans off books before audit.

5

Get Everything in Writing

Never settle on a phone call. Insist on formal Settlement Letter signed by the bank.

6

Move "Settled" to "Closed"

After paying, request status change from "Settled" to "Closed" in CIBIL. This improves your credit score significantly.

"Settled" vs "Closed": Why It Matters for CIBIL

This is critical: After OTS, your CIBIL shows "Settled" status, which damages your credit for 5-7 years. You CAN change this:

Status Meaning CIBIL Impact Can Change?
Active Paying on time Excellent (750+) Keep paying
Closed Fully paid/closed Good (650-700+) No (Good!)
Settled Partial payment agreed Poor (500-600) YES! Request change
Written Off Bank gave up Very Poor (300-400) No (Worse)
Pro Strategy: After paying your OTS amount, send the bank a letter requesting status change from "Settled" to "Closed". Many banks do this. This single change can improve your CIBIL by 50-100 points.
