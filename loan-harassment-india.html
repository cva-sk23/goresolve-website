This page covers borrower rights and how to respond to loan harassment in India.

Updated: April 10, 2026 Reading Time: 5 min

What Counts as Loan Harassment in India?

Loan harassment occurs when banks, NBFCs, or their recovery agents use illegal methods to pressure borrowers into repaying. Under RBI's Fair Practice Code and the 2022 Digital Lending Guidelines, borrowers have clear legal protections against such behaviour.

All of the following are illegal under RBI rules: calls before 7AM or after 7PM, abusive language, threats of arrest, contacting family members, visiting home without notice, and public shaming on WhatsApp or social media.

Your Rights as a Borrower in India

  • Right to dignity — recovery agents must treat you respectfully at all times
  • Right to privacy — your debt is private; agents cannot tell your family, neighbours, or employer
  • Right to reasonable contact hours — 7AM to 7PM only, no exceptions
  • Right to know the agent's identity — they must show authorisation from the lender
  • Right to request written communication — you can demand all contact be in writing
  • Right to negotiate settlement — banks must consider genuine hardship cases
  • Right to file complaint — at RBI, Consumer Court, or police without fear of retaliation

How to Stop Loan Harassment Immediately

1

Document everything first

Save all call recordings, screenshots of threatening messages, WhatsApp communications, and a log of call timings. Date and time of each incident matters.

2

Send written complaint to bank's nodal officer

Every bank and NBFC must have a nodal officer for grievances. A written complaint with evidence often stops harassment within 48 hours — they don't want regulatory trouble.

3

File RBI complaint at cms.rbi.org.in

Free, online, and taken seriously. Once a complaint is registered, the lender is formally put on notice. Harassment typically stops during the investigation.

4

File police FIR if threats were made

If the agent threatened physical harm, used criminal intimidation language, or threatened false cases — file an FIR at your local police station under IPC 503 and 506.

5

Negotiate settlement in parallel

Stopping harassment is step one. Resolving the debt is step two. Approaching the lender with a realistic OTS offer while the complaint is pending puts you in the strongest position.

Remember: Loan default is a civil matter in India — not a crime. You cannot be arrested for not paying a personal loan or credit card. Anyone threatening arrest is lying and violating the law.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can recovery agents call my family members?

No. Under RBI's 2022 Digital Lending Guidelines, recovery agents cannot contact your family, friends, neighbours or employer about your debt. This is a direct RBI violation.

What if harassment continues after filing a complaint?

Escalate to the RBI Ombudsman and simultaneously file a police FIR. If harassment continues during an active RBI complaint, the lender faces severe penalties.

Can I record calls with recovery agents?

Yes. In India, single-party consent recording is permitted — meaning you can record a call you are participating in. These recordings are valid as evidence in complaints.
