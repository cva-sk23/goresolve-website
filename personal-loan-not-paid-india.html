Personal Loan Not Paid India

This page explains what to do if a personal loan remains unpaid in India and borrower rights.

Updated: April 10, 2026 Reading Time: 5 min

What Happens If Personal Loan is Not Paid in India?

Missing personal loan EMIs triggers a series of steps by the bank or NBFC — starting with calls and notices, and potentially escalating to legal action over time. Here is exactly what happens at each stage and what you can do.

Days Past DueWhat Bank DoesYour Status
1–30 daysReminder calls, SMS, emailsSMA-0 (Special Mention Account)
31–60 daysIncreased calls, field visit possibleSMA-1
61–90 daysRecovery agents deployedSMA-2
90+ daysLoan classified as NPANon-Performing Asset
6–12 monthsLegal notice, settlement offerNPA — settlement window open
12+ monthsCourt case filing possibleNPA — legal action risk

Important window: The 3–12 month period is your best time to negotiate OTS. The loan has become NPA, the bank wants resolution, and they are most flexible on settlement amount.

Legal Consequences of Not Paying Personal Loan

  • CIBIL score drops — every missed EMI reduces your score; NPA classification drops it significantly
  • Recovery agent harassment — legal under RBI rules within limits; illegal harassment is a separate issue
  • Legal notice — bank may send a formal legal notice through a lawyer
  • Civil court case — for unsecured loans, bank files civil suit; process takes 1–3 years
  • Bank account attachment — if court grants decree, bank can attach your accounts

What cannot happen: You cannot be arrested for not paying a personal loan. Loan default is a civil matter in India — not a criminal offence. Anyone threatening arrest is lying.

What to Do If You Cannot Pay Personal Loan EMI

1

Contact your bank immediately — before missing EMI

Proactive communication changes everything. If you anticipate missing an EMI, call your bank and explain. Many banks offer moratoriums, restructuring, or grace periods for genuine hardship cases.

2

Request loan restructuring

RBI mandates banks to offer restructuring to borrowers facing genuine financial stress. Request an extended tenure, reduced EMI, or interest moratorium in writing.

3

Apply for OTS once loan becomes NPA

Once 90+ days past due, your loan is NPA. This is when to approach the bank with an OTS offer. Banks typically accept 40–60% of principal outstanding for personal loans.

4

Stop illegal harassment while negotiating

You have the right to negotiate settlement AND stop illegal recovery practices simultaneously. File an RBI complaint for any harassment while continuing settlement talks.

What Happens If Online Loan is Not Paid in India?

For digital loans and app-based lending, the same consequences apply — but with additional risks. Many digital lenders use aggressive tactics including contacting your phone contacts, sending threatening messages, or morphing your photos. All of these are illegal under RBI's 2022 Digital Lending Guidelines.

If an online lender is harassing you, file an immediate complaint at cms.rbi.org.in. RBI has penalised multiple digital lenders for these practices.

Personal Loan Settlement Percentage — What Banks Accept

Banks typically accept the following OTS percentages for personal loans:

  • 0–6 months NPA: 70–85% of total outstanding
  • 6–12 months NPA: 50–70% of principal
  • 12–24 months NPA: 40–60% of principal
  • 24+ months NPA (write-off): 30–50% of principal

Frequently Asked Questions

Personal loan not paid — what will happen in India?

Your loan becomes NPA after 90 days. CIBIL score drops, recovery agents are deployed, and eventually a legal notice may be sent. You cannot be arrested. You can negotiate OTS at any stage.

What happens if online loan is not paid in India?

Same as any personal loan — civil consequences, CIBIL impact, recovery contact. Digital lenders cannot access your contacts or use threatening tactics. File RBI complaint for any such behaviour.

Can I get loan written off?

Banks write off loans internally after extended NPA periods. This does not mean you are free — the bank can still pursue recovery. However, it often leads to more flexible OTS offers.
