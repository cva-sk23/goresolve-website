Stop Loan Harassment in India

Are you receiving constant calls, threats, or pressure from loan recovery agents? You are not alone — and you have legal rights.

What recovery agents can legally do

  • Contact you during reasonable hours
  • Request repayment politely
  • Follow RBI guidelines

What recovery agents CANNOT do

  • Threaten or abuse you
  • Call repeatedly at odd hours
  • Harass your family or workplace
