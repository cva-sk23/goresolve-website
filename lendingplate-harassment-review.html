Is LendingPlate Harassing You?

LendingPlate is an NBFC-backed digital lending platform offering personal loans. Like many digital lenders, borrower complaints about aggressive recovery practices have increased sharply. If you are facing calls at odd hours, threats, or contacts to your family — you have legal protection under RBI rules.

Know this: LendingPlate is regulated by RBI. Their recovery agents must follow RBI's Fair Practice Code. Any violation is a complaint-worthy offence.

Common LendingPlate Harassment Complaints

  • Calls before 7AM or after 7PM
  • Abusive or threatening language from agents
  • Contacting family members, employers, or neighbours
  • WhatsApp messages with morphed images or public shaming
  • Threatening arrest or police action (which is illegal for loan default)
  • Multiple calls per day causing mental stress
  • Demands for payment to unknown accounts

How to Stop LendingPlate Harassment — Step by Step

1

Document every incident

Save call recordings, screenshots of messages, and note down dates and times of every harassment incident. This is your evidence.

2

Write to LendingPlate's Nodal Officer

Send a formal written complaint to their nodal officer via email. State the violations clearly with evidence. This creates a paper trail and often stops harassment within days.

3

File RBI Complaint at cms.rbi.org.in

Completely free. Select LendingPlate's parent NBFC from the list. Describe the harassment with dates. RBI takes NBFC harassment complaints seriously.

4

Negotiate a settlement

While filing the complaint, also negotiate a realistic settlement. Banks and NBFCs prefer settlement over prolonged disputes. GOresolve can help you negotiate the right amount.

Pro tip: Filing an RBI complaint and negotiating settlement simultaneously is the most effective strategy. The complaint creates pressure; the settlement offer gives them a way out.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is LendingPlate a legal company?

Yes. LendingPlate operates under RBI-registered NBFC partnerships. Being legal does not mean their recovery practices are always compliant — violations can and should be reported.

Can LendingPlate sue me for not paying?

Yes, they can file a civil case. But this is expensive and time-consuming for them. Most NBFCs prefer settlement. Engage proactively before it reaches court.

What if they contact my family?

This is a direct violation of RBI's 2022 Digital Lending Guidelines. File an immediate complaint at cms.rbi.org.in and to their nodal officer. This is one of the strongest grounds for complaint.
