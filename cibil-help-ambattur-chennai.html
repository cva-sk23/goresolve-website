Ambattur — Industrial Zone Debt & CIBIL Challenges

Ambattur is one of Tamil Nadu's largest industrial zones — home to garment factories, electronics manufacturers, and engineering units. The workforce here is predominantly organised sector employees whose loan eligibility was strong at the time of borrowing but whose repayment capacity has been affected by layoffs, factory closures, and income disruptions.

CIBIL damage in Ambattur most commonly comes from EMI defaults on personal loans and credit cards taken during employment, that could not be continued after job changes or factory relocations. GOresolve's approach: first settle the NPA account through OTS, then build a structured 12-month CIBIL recovery plan. Both steps are necessary — settlement alone without a rebuild plan leaves your credit profile vulnerable.
